I am an economist at the [University of Fribourg](https://www.unifr.ch/econ/en/) (Switzerland), where I completed my PhD in Economics under the supervision of [Mark Schelker](https://www.unifr.ch/econ/en/). <!-- TODO: check the link and the wording (e.g. "PhD candidate" vs. "completed") -->

My research lies in **urban and regional economics** and **economic geography**. I study how infrastructure, place-based policies, distance, and borders shape where firms locate, how they are created and disappear, and how they are connected to each other.

To do so, I built a new historical database by digitizing the *Répertoire des administrateurs des sociétés anonymes suisses*, a registry listing all Swiss limited companies and their directors from 1934 to 2003. It allows me to follow the Swiss corporate landscape and its corporate network at the municipal level over six decades.

My research focuses on:

- **Transport infrastructure** and firm dynamics (creation, death, and relocation of firms)
- **Place-based policies** and their local and spatial effects
- **Corporate networks**, interlocking directorates, and internal border effects
- Causal inference with **staggered difference-in-differences** and **gravity models**
